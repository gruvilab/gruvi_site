  <div class="about-intro__text">
    <h1>About</h1>
    <p class="about-intro__lead">GrUVi is an interdisciplinary team of researchers in visual computing, computer graphics and computer vision, in the School of Computing Science at Simon Fraser University.</p>
    <p>Professor Tom Calvert founded the lab in 1992. His work in human animation led to the LifeForms software and the startup Credo Interactive. Today the lab’s infrastructure is supported by the Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI) grants and NSERC Research Tools and Instruments grants.</p>
    <p class="about-intro__place">TASC 8004 · Burnaby campus</p>
  </div>
</section>
